# DUO｜双人互动版最终生图提示词

必须事先核实双人姿势来源：有来源则传入姿势图片／ID；没有则标识「原创互动动作」而不是编造双人 PoseWear 编号。

```text
POSEWEAR RANDOM / DUO — single coherent editorial photograph with EXACTLY TWO ADULT subjects.

Person A at [左/右及前后位置]: facial identity, hairstyle, adult appearance and personality from [人像A图片/ID]. Outfit A from [服装A图片/ID], preserving actual garment cut, layers, textiles, color, length, shoes and bag.

Person B at [左/右及前后位置]: visibly different adult face, hairstyle and personality from [人像B图片/ID]. Outfit B from [服装B图片/ID]. No swapping of clothes or faces, no merged identity.

DUO POSE: [真实网站双人姿势图/ID 或明确“自定义动作”] is the decisive skeleton. Recreate torso direction, eye lines, hand contacts, shoulders, hips, legs and foot positions for BOTH people; physical interaction is specific: [挽臂/靠肩/并肩旋转/其他], with [A哪只手接触B哪里] and [B的回应动作]. Body weights, contact points, fabric tension, hair occlusion and background perspectives must be consistent. NOT a static side-by-side group photo unless the reference explicitly dictates it.

Location selected from both outfits and personalities: [实际可行的室内/户外场景]. Lighting [主光/柔和补光], coherent shadow direction, background genuinely far enough for optical bokeh. Simulated Nikon Z8 or Nikon Z9, NIKKOR Z 50–85mm portrait perspective, f/2.8–f/4 with both faces in focus, lifelike textured skin and fabric, premium 9:16 vertical fashion photography, natural color and fine photographic detail.

Frame both people with complete essential limbs and shoes, exactly two faces and four hands, realistic fingers and credible proportions. No extra person, no swapped identities, no collage, no faux lens blur, no text, no watermark, no toy-like or synthetic appearance.
```

**修正顺序：** 人数 → A/B 服装和人脸归属 → 双人接触关系 → 手脚结构 → 焦点/布光/背景。避免靠改变动作来“修图”。

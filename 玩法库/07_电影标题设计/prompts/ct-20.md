## 可复制英文提示词

Create a finished [FORMAT] in [ASPECT_RATIO] for the exact title "[TITLE]". Typography direction: Condensed Sans. Letter design: tightly spaced tall grotesk; small security metadata away from letters. Composition: Compact pale block capitals sit against dark civic architecture with sparse horizontal signal artifacts. Scene: empty government corridor, overhead cold bars. Color and light: charcoal, dirty white. Adapt title scale and line breaks to the supplied wording and format. Keep exact spelling and believable cinematic depth. Independently design the lettering, objects and shot. Output one image.

## 使用
将 TITLE 换为用户原文，FORMAT 换为本次用途，ASPECT_RATIO 换为所需比例。用户未指定时沿用该款横幅构图。DMIAO 是当前仓库预览统一使用的文字。生成后核对标题、字形、占比、取景与光线。

## 排版和动态
后期排版可从 Oswald 开始，再做字距、轮廓和材质设计。上游许可见[字体清单](../fonts/verified-ofl-candidates.csv)。实际预览的文字随 AI 图像生成，具体字形及变化以成图为准。可编辑需求应另交付标题图层和底图。动态需求可从下述节奏展开：flicker of one offscreen light only; 6s。仅在用户请求时制作动态。

## 可复制英文提示词

Create a finished [FORMAT] in [ASPECT_RATIO] for the exact title "[TITLE]". Typography direction: Fractured Serif. Letter design: wide cold serif capitals split by independently placed glass crack. Composition: Split two lines of thin silver serif lettering with a diagonal fracture on rainy glass; silhouettes remain behind the pane. Scene: rain on window, anonymous shadow, deep city reflections. Color and light: steel blue, dim red. Adapt title scale and line breaks to the supplied wording and format. Keep exact spelling and believable cinematic depth. Independently design the lettering, objects and shot. Output one image.

## 使用
将 TITLE 换为用户原文，FORMAT 换为本次用途，ASPECT_RATIO 换为所需比例。用户未指定时沿用该款横幅构图。DMIAO 是当前仓库预览统一使用的文字。生成后核对标题、字形、占比、取景与光线。

## 排版和动态
后期排版可从 Cormorant Garamond 开始，再做字距、轮廓和材质设计。上游许可见[字体清单](../fonts/verified-ofl-candidates.csv)。实际预览的文字随 AI 图像生成，具体字形及变化以成图为准。可编辑需求应另交付标题图层和底图。动态需求可从下述节奏展开：one raindrop follows crack and distorts reflection; 7s。仅在用户请求时制作动态。

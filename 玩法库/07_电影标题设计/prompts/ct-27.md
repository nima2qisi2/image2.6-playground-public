## 可复制英文提示词

Create a finished [FORMAT] in [ASPECT_RATIO] for the exact title "[TITLE]". Typography direction: Minimal Sci-Fi Sans. Letter design: thin wide-set geometric capitals with original open strokes and a smaller tracked second line. Composition: Center a wide single line of slender champagne-bronze geometric capitals with original open strokes. Place the smaller tracked second line below, low dunes along the bottom and a huge copper planetary limb entering from the right. Keep the sky dark and the title dominant. Scene: low dunes in deep shadow; a giant copper-lit planetary limb enters from the right. Color and light: copper, midnight. Adapt title scale and line breaks to the supplied wording and format. Keep exact spelling and believable cinematic depth. Independently design the lettering, objects and shot. Output one image.

## 使用
将 TITLE 换为用户原文，FORMAT 换为本次用途，ASPECT_RATIO 换为所需比例。用户未指定时沿用该款横幅构图。DMIAO 是当前仓库预览统一使用的文字。生成后核对标题、字形、占比、取景与光线。

## 排版和动态
后期排版可从 Syne 开始，再做字距、轮廓和材质设计。上游许可见[字体清单](../fonts/verified-ofl-candidates.csv)。实际预览的文字随 AI 图像生成，具体字形及变化以成图为准。可编辑需求应另交付标题图层和底图。动态需求可从下述节奏展开：slow giant shadow moves across valley; 9s。仅在用户请求时制作动态。
